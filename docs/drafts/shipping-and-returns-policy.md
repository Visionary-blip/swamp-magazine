## Shipping

- **Shipping — $5 flat rate**, no matter how many pieces are in the order.

**When it ships**

Each drop is made in a small run. Orders are packed and sent within
**[3–5 business days]** of the order, and you'll get a tracking number by email
when it goes out. Delivery usually takes **[2–5 business days]** after that,
depending on the carrier. Carrier delays are outside our control.

**Address and delivery**

Please check your shipping address before you pay. If a package comes back to us
because of a wrong or incomplete address, we'll contact you to re-send it at your
cost **[or: refund the order minus shipping]**. If your tracking shows delivered
and you can't find the package, email us and we'll help look into it.

**Order limits**

Drops are limited. Stock is checked when you check out. If a size or the whole
drop sells out while your payment is going through, we'll refund you in full and

## Returns & exchanges

- Return within **[14 days]** of delivery (or pickup).
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition, with any tags.
- Email **hello@swampmagazine.com** with your name, order, and what you're
  returning. We'll reply with where to send it.
- **[The buyer pays return shipping.]** Original shipping ($5) isn't refunded.
- Once it arrives and we check it, we'll refund the item to your original payment
  method within **[5–10 business days]**. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
- Campus pickup orders can be returned in person by arrangement, same window.

- **Wrong or damaged item:** email us within **[7 days]** of receiving it with a
  photo. We'll send the right one or refund you, and cover the shipping.
- **Exchanges:** **[Sizes are limited, so exchanges depend on what's in stock. Email
  us and we'll see what we can do.]**
- **Add-ons** (small extras bought with an order) **[follow the same rules / are
  final sale]**.
- **Cancelling an order:** email us before it ships and we'll cancel and refund it.
